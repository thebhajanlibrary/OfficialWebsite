# Caribistani kitchen vocabulary: dictionary cross-check

Reference: **Guyanese Hindi Dictionary**, by **Kaveeta Sharma**, eight-page PDF supplied by the project owner as `Guyanese+Hindi+Dictionary.pdf`. The document displays `www.rajivmohabir.com`. Page numbers below refer to the PDF's numbered pages.

The owner's illustrated sheet supplies the primary labels and utensil designs. The dictionary supplies supporting meanings and additional accepted names. Its Guyanese scope does not establish that every variant is used throughout the Caribbean.

The umbrella term **bartan** (page 1) introduces cooking utensils/vessels.

The dictionary does not independently confirm the sheet's **Chīmmach**, **Hanriyā**, **Dablā**, or the full **Okhṛī aur …** label. Those remain based on the supplied image. The cropped final label has not been completed by guesswork. **Karahi** on page 5 is not used as an alternate name for the pictured hanriyā.

Dictionary variants appear on study cards and are accepted in typed recall. Multiple-choice questions retain the sheet's primary labels. Image designs are unchanged. The PDF itself is not republished.
